Trust comes first

Privacy and policy safeguards

This public GitHub Pages demo is a conversation aid. It is not an official UW–Whitewater system, decision process, or policy interpretation.

01

Fictional examples only

No file upload, login, analytics, network submission, or backend. Tool inputs stay in the current browser tab and clear when refreshed.

02

Classify before using data

Use public or approved, de-identified examples only in a public demo. Treat education records, personal information, unpublished research, internal materials, and confidential or proprietary data as restricted from public AI tools.

03

Human review stays in charge

AI may support drafting or organizing an approved process; it must not make admissions, grading, eligibility, or other consequential decisions. Use approved university systems and authorized reviewers.

04

Ask the right office

For uncertain data handling or disclosure, pause and consult the Registrar, Information Technology Services, privacy/security contacts, or the responsible policy owner before proceeding.

Verified reference points

  • UW–Whitewater: Allowable and Prohibited AI Uses — AI use is governed by Universities of Wisconsin, Board of Regents, and federal privacy/security laws.
  • UW–Whitewater: Faculty and Staff AI Guidelines — protect personal and university information; do not upload student work without explicit consent.
  • UW–Whitewater FERPA guidance — education records are protected; when disclosure is uncertain, consult the Registrar.
  • UW–Whitewater information security policies — institutional policy reference hub.

Policy links checked November 18, 2026. Confirm current policy, data classification, contracts, and approvals with UW–Whitewater and Universities of Wisconsin before any operational use.
